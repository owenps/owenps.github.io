I had written a programming style guide about a year ago for [Ledge](ledge.io). Here are a particular excerpt that I wish to keep.

> Another word for style is design.
>
> Good design feels familiar, it's when all components sing to the same tune. Regardless of where you are in the codebase, it should feel familiar. As if one mind has written every line of code.
>
> Consistent style both directly and indirectly guides the decisions we make. Significantly reducing the guesswork and cognitive friction needed to deliver new features and improve existing ones.
>
> Fundamentally good style is good communication, and poor style is poor communication.
